Model state

This model is NOT deductive: it doesn't hold counted votes fixed and project only the remainder. Every county's projection blends its own results with the baseline at a credibility weight that grows with how much has reported -- and shifts further toward observed results when many counties show the same pattern, not just one. Sherburne County's baseline is a statewide-average placeholder, not a real read -- treat its numbers with extra skepticism.
